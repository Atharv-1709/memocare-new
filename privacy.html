Plain-language privacy notice

Your MemoCare information belongs to you

Guest mode

Medicines, people, memories, routines, safe places, settings, and device logs stay in this browser profile. MemoCare does not silently send them to a caregiver or cloud service.

Sign-in

Firebase sign-in can identify you after the project owner configures it. Sign-in by itself does not upload your health or memory records. A separate, authorized sync backend is required.

Location, camera, microphone, and serial device

MemoCare asks for these permissions only when you choose a related action. Continuous location is off by default. Arduino serial messages stay between this browser and the selected USB device.

Exports

An export may contain sensitive health, contact, location, and memory information. Keep the JSON file private and import only files you trust.

Your choices

You can export, import, or permanently delete all on-device MemoCare data in Settings. Browser site-data controls can also remove local data.

Safety

MemoCare is an assistance prototype. It does not prescribe medicine, verify pills, guarantee alerts, or replace clinicians, caregivers, or emergency services.
